About & method

Why this site exists, and how we check what it says.

Why this site exists

Many Americans grow up believing the United States won World War II in Europe more or less on its own. Polls show it: in 2024, 59% of Americans said the US did most to defeat the Nazis. This site exists to tell a bigger story, in which American industry and courage matter enormously, alongside the Red Army, the British Empire and the fighters of occupied Europe.

It is not anti-American, and it is not pro-Russian. It corrects myths on every side, including the Soviet ones, and it gives the American contribution its full weight.

How we check facts

  • Every figure has a source. Each article lists its sources, and we prefer primary documents, official histories and recognized historians.
  • We say when numbers are disputed. Where sources disagree, as with Soviet casualties or Lend-Lease aircraft, we give ranges or name the source we use.
  • We flag weak quotes. Famous lines with no primary source, like "English time, American money, Russian blood", are not used as evidence.
  • We give the other side. Every article ends with a "fair version" that states the strongest counter-argument.
