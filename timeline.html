1939–1945, front by front

Filter the war by who was fighting. Notice how much happens before the United States enters it.

  1. Sep 1, 1939Other AlliesGermany invades Poland

    Britain and France declare war two days later.

  2. Sep 17, 1939Eastern FrontThe USSR invades eastern Poland

    Under the secret protocol of the Molotov–Ribbentrop pact.

  3. Nov 1939United StatesCash-and-carry

    The US allows arms sales to nations at war, if paid in cash and carried on their own ships.

  4. Feb 11, 1940Eastern FrontSoviet–German trade agreement

    Soviet oil and grain begin flowing to Germany.

  5. Jun 10 & 13, 1940United StatesFrance appeals to Roosevelt

    Reynaud asks for an immediate US entry into the war.

  6. Jun 22, 1940Western FrontFrance signs an armistice

    Nearly 60,000 French soldiers had been killed in six weeks.

  7. Jul – Oct 1940Western FrontBattle of Britain

    The RAF, one pilot in five foreign, defeats the Luftwaffe.

  8. Sep 2, 1940United StatesDestroyers for bases

    50 US destroyers go to Britain in exchange for base leases.

  9. Mar 11, 1941United StatesLend-Lease becomes law

    The US becomes the arsenal of the Allies.

  10. Apr – Aug 1941Other AlliesSiege of Tobruk

    Up to 14,000 Australians hold the port in Libya.

  11. Jun 22, 1941Eastern FrontGermany invades the USSR

    99 of 190 German divisions attack in the East.

  12. Oct 31, 1941United StatesUSS Reuben James sunk

    The undeclared naval war in the Atlantic claims a US destroyer.

  13. Dec 1941Eastern FrontGermany stopped before Moscow

    The Soviet counter-offensive pushes the Wehrmacht back.

  14. Dec 7 & 11, 1941United StatesPearl Harbor, then Hitler's declaration

    Germany declares war on the US; Congress replies the same day.

  15. Aug 1942 – Feb 1943Eastern FrontStalingrad

    The German Sixth Army is encircled and destroyed.

  16. Jul – Aug 1943Eastern FrontKursk

    Germany's last great offensive in the East fails.

  17. May 11–12, 1944Other AlliesGarigliano breakthrough

    French colonial troops open the road to Rome.

  18. Jun 6, 1944Western FrontD-Day

    US, British, Canadian and other Allied forces land in Normandy.

  19. Jun 22, 1944Eastern FrontOperation Bagration

    The Red Army destroys German Army Group Centre.

  20. Aug 1, 1944Other AlliesWarsaw Uprising

    45,000 Home Army fighters rise; the Red Army halts across the Vistula.

  21. Aug 15, 1944Western FrontProvence landing

    French Army B forms most of the force; Toulon and Marseille fall early.

  22. Oct 20, 1944Other AlliesBelgrade liberated

    By Yugoslav partisans and the Red Army.

  23. Autumn 1944Western FrontBattle of the Scheldt

    Canadians open the port of Antwerp.

  24. Dec 1944 – Jan 1945United StatesBattle of the Bulge

    Over 600,000 Americans engaged; 19,246 killed.

  25. Apr – May 1945Eastern FrontBattle of Berlin

    Soviet and Polish troops take the capital.

  26. May 8, 1945Other AlliesVictory in Europe

    Germany surrenders unconditionally.
