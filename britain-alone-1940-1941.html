Britain alone: the year no one else fought

For twelve months after the fall of France, the British Empire was the only major power at war with Hitler. The USSR was still his partner. The US was still neutral.

6 min readSourced and fact-checked
Short answerBetween the fall of France in June 1940 and the German invasion of the USSR in June 1941, Britain and its empire fought Germany and Italy without a major ally. They won the Battle of Britain, kept the Atlantic open and fought in Africa and the Mediterranean, while the US supplied aid but stayed out of the war.

A war no other great power was fighting

In June 1940, France signed an armistice. The Soviet Union was bound to Germany by the 1939 pact and was supplying it with oil and grain. The United States was neutral. Britain, together with Canada, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, India and exiles from occupied Europe, was the only force still fighting.

The Battle of Britain was won by a coalition

From July to October 1940, the Royal Air Force defeated the Luftwaffe's attempt to win control of the skies over England, which was the condition for a German invasion. Of the 2,936 aircrew who flew in the battle, 595 were not British.

The Polish No. 303 Squadron was the highest-scoring unit of the battle, with 126 claimed victories. Claims on both sides were later revised downwards, so the numbers are best read as relative.

A codebreaking head start from Poland

Britain's later success in reading German Enigma traffic at Bletchley Park started in Warsaw. In July 1939, at a meeting in the Pyry forest, Polish cryptologists who had been breaking Enigma since 1933 handed their methods to the British and French. According to GCHQ and the UK government's history blog, it saved Britain at least a year.

The empire at war

The war was fought by the whole empire. In 1941, up to 14,000 Australian troops held the besieged port of Tobruk in Libya from April to August, months before Pearl Harbor. The Indian Army grew from about 205,000 men in 1939 to more than 3.35 million by 1945, often described as the largest volunteer army in history. New Zealand lost a higher share of its population than any other Commonwealth country.

The fair versionBritain did not fight 1940–41 without American help: the 50 destroyers of September 1940 and Lend-Lease from March 1941 were vital. And the empire's war had a darker side, including the Bengal famine of 1943. But for a year, it was British and Commonwealth soldiers, sailors and airmen who did the fighting.

Frequently asked questions

Did Britain fight Germany alone?

From June 1940 to June 1941, Britain and its empire were the only major power at war with Germany, with help from exiled Polish, Czech, French, Belgian, Dutch and Norwegian forces and American aid.

How many American pilots flew in the Battle of Britain?

Nine, according to the official roll of aircrew. Polish pilots were the largest foreign group, with 145.

Who broke the Enigma code first?

Polish cryptologists Marian Rejewski, Jerzy Różycki and Henryk Zygalski, from 1933. They shared their methods with Britain and France in July 1939.

When did the Soviet Union and the US join the war against Germany?

The Soviet Union after Germany invaded it on June 22, 1941. The United States on December 11, 1941, after Germany declared war on it.
